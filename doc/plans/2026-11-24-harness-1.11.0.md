## Tasks

### Task 1 — `pal-harness cpu`

- **Repo and files:**
  - new package `cpu/`: `cpu.go`, `read_windows.go`, `read_linux.go`, `read_other.go`,
    `cpu_test.go`;
  - `cmd/pal-harness/cpu_cmd.go` and `cpu_cmd_test.go`;
  - `cmd/pal-harness/main.go` (usage line and dispatch).
- **Test first:**
  - `cpu_test.go`: the busy share from two samples (pinning the Windows kernel-includes-idle rule);
    `/proc/stat` parsing; `Check` quiet, busy, busy then quiet within `Wait`, and a read error;
  - `cpu_cmd_test.go`: exit 0, 1 and 2; bad `--max` (not a percent, over 100), negative `--wait`,
    zero `--for`, extra arguments.
- **Code:**
  - `cpu.Sample` holds `{Idle, Total}`;
  - `Busy(a, b)` returns a percent;
  - `Check(Options{Max, For, Wait, Every, Read, Now, Sleep}, out)` prints `cpu busy: N% over 10s (maximum 25%): ok|busy`
    and mirrors `mem.Check`'s wait loop;
  - the readers: Windows `GetSystemTimes`, Linux `/proc/stat`, other platforms an error.
- **Prove it:** `go test ./cpu ./cmd/pal-harness` pass; `GOOS=linux go vet ./...` and
  `GOOS=darwin go vet ./...` clean.
